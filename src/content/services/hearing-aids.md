## Types of Hearing Aids

**Based on Style/Design**
1. **Behind-The-Ear (BTE):** Rests behind the ear with sound routed into the canal.
2. **Receiver-In-Canal (RIC) / Receiver-In-The-Ear (RITE):** Smaller than BTEs, with the speaker sitting directly in the ear canal for natural sound.
3. **In-The-Ear (ITE):** Custom molded to sit in the outer portion of the ear canal.
4. **In-The-Canal (ITC):** Custom molded to sit in the canal, slightly smaller than ITE.
5. **Completely-In-Canal (CIC):** A tiny shell that fits entirely inside the ear canal.
6. **Invisible-In-Canal (IIC):** The smallest custom-made shell that fits deep into the ear canal, making it virtually invisible.

**Based on Technology**
1. **Analog Hearing Aids:** Basic amplification with minimal processing.
2. **Digital Hearing Aids:** Advanced sound processing for clarity and noise reduction.
3. **AI Hearing Aids (Artificial Intelligence):** Hearing aids that learn your environment, automatically adapting to background noise and focusing on speech.

**Based on Power Source**
1. **Rechargeable Hearing Aids:** Built-in advanced Li-ion batteries providing all-day power.
2. **Disposable Battery Hearing Aids:** Uses standard replaceable batteries.

**Special Types of Hearing Aids**
1. **CROS Hearing Aids:** For single-sided deafness, routing sound from the bad ear to the good ear.
2. **BiCROS Hearing Aids:** For bilateral asymmetrical hearing loss.
3. **Bone Conduction Hearing Devices:** Transmits sound via bone vibration directly to the inner ear.
4. **Tinnitus Management Device:** Specialized devices that generate masking sounds to relieve tinnitus.

## Modern <span class="text-primary">Advancements</span>
Modern hearing aids have undergone continuous augmentation to become smarter and more autonomous:
- **Artificial Intelligence (AI):** Hearing aids that learn your environment, automatically dampening background noise and focusing on speech without manual adjustments.
- **Wireless Connectivity:** 2.4 GHz Bluetooth v5 LE allows you to stream music, TV, and phone calls directly to your hearing aids.
- **Rechargeability:** Advanced Li-ion batteries provide all-day power, eliminating the need to constantly change tiny batteries.
- **Aggressive Noise Reduction:** Features like Speech Enhancer and StereoZoom actively suppress noise from the sides and back, helping you hear clearly in bustling restaurants.
