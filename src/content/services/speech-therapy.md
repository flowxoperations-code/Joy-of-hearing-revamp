Our speech and language assessment and rehabilitation services help individuals of all ages <span class="text-primary font-semibold">overcome communication and feeding challenges.</span> 

## Speech Disorders
We treat abnormalities in sound production, including:
- **Articulation Disorders:** Difficulty making sounds in syllables or saying words correctly, making speech difficult to understand.
- **Fluency Disorders (Stuttering):** Interruptions in the normal flow or rate of speech.
- **Voice/Resonance Disorders:** Abnormalities in pitch, loudness, or vocal quality that can impact confidence and communication ease.

## Language and Feeding Disorders
- **Language Disorders:** Difficulties in understanding others (receptive) or sharing thoughts and ideas (expressive).
- **Feeding/Swallowing Disorders (Dysphagia):** Difficulties in the normal way of eating or swallowing, often secondary to other medical conditions or anatomical issues like cleft palate.

## Our <span class="text-primary">Intervention</span> Services
Early intervention is critical, as the brain's ability to adapt is highest during early development. Our certified Speech-Language Pathologists (SLPs) provide:
- Comprehensive speech, language, and voice assessments.
- One-on-one and group therapy sessions.
- Specialized Auditory-Verbal Therapy (AVT) for cochlear implant recipients.
- Neurological speech and language disorder management (e.g., post-stroke recovery).
