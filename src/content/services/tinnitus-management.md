Tinnitus is the perception of sound—often a ringing, buzzing, humming, or whooshing—without an external source. It is not an illness itself, but a <span class="text-primary font-semibold">symptom of an underlying condition</span> in the auditory system.

## The Impact of Tinnitus
Tinnitus is more than just a ringing in the ears. It can significantly disrupt everyday life, leading to:
- Insomnia and fatigue.
- Difficulty concentrating or relaxing.
- Despair, frustration, and anxiety.
- Headaches and stress.

## Common Causes & Triggers
While the exact mechanisms can be complex, tinnitus is often triggered or worsened by:
- **Hearing Loss:** Tinnitus can be the brain's attempt to "fill in the gaps" of sounds you can no longer hear.
- **Loud Noise Exposure:** Damage to the inner ear hair cells from loud environments.
- **Ear Infections:** Temporary tinnitus can result from congestion or fluid buildup.
- **Stress & Anxiety:** High stress levels can make tinnitus significantly more noticeable.
- **Other Factors:** Medications, Meniere's disease, vascular disorders, or caffeine/nicotine use.
